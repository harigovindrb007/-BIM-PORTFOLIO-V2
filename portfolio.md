# Hari Govind R.B - BIM Engineering Portfolio

## Project 1: ONGC Oil & Gas Facility (250 Acres)
**Location:** Uran, Maharashtra, India  
**Sector:** Industrial / Oil & Gas  
**Role:** BIM Engineer (Project Associate L1)  
**Tools Used:** Autodesk Revit, FARO (Point Cloud Data)  

### 1. The Context & Challenge
The ONGC Uran facility is a massive 250-acre oil and gas plant. Following an on-site accident, there was a critical need to assess the structural stability of the affected areas to facilitate safe reconstruction. The primary challenge was accurately capturing the complex, real-world conditions of the damaged industrial steel structures, pipe racks, and storage tanks so that structural engineers could reliably analyze the plant's health.

### 2. My Process (Scan-to-BIM)
I was responsible for delivering the large-scale point cloud-based steel modeling for the structural health assessment. 
*   **Scan-to-BIM Conversion:** Using as-built FARO scan data, I reverse-engineered and modeled the complex industrial steel structures in Revit. 
*   **Dimensional Fidelity:** It was critical to maintain precise dimensional accuracy against the point cloud data to ensure the structural engineers had a true "digital twin" for their condition assessment.

### 3. The Deliverable & Impact
*   **Accurate As-Built Models:** Delivered highly detailed structural models of the facility's tanks, sheds, and support trestles.
*   **Structural Documentation:** Produced accurate structural layout sheets, plans, sections, and elevations directly from the point cloud-verified model.
*   **Outcome:** These deliverables successfully provided the engineering teams with the exact geometric baseline required for their detailed structural stability reports and the subsequent reconstruction of the plant.

## Project 3: EXIT 15 – Rainscreen Facade Cladding
**Location:** Al Nahda Entertainment Complex (Water Park) - Riyadh, Saudi Arabia  
**Sector:** Entertainment / Leisure  
**Role:** BIM Team Lead  
**Tools Used:** Revit, Navisworks Manage, ACC  

### 1. The Context & Challenge
For the Water Park area of the EXIT 15 entertainment complex, I modeled the extensive rainscreen cladding and curtain wall facade systems. The primary challenge was working from IFC specifications and design intent drawings to maintain geometry-data fidelity across hundreds of custom, organic curvilinear facade panels, while coordinating with structural steel framing and thermal barrier sub-assemblies.

### 2. My Process & Coordination
*   **Parametric Curtain Panel Families:** Developed and deployed parametric curtain panel families adhering strictly to IFC model requirements, embedding fabrication parameters, tolerance allowances, and CNC cut profiles.
*   **Inter-Discipline Clash Resolution:** Collaborated closely with the structural steel team in Navisworks Manage to detect and resolve facade-to-structure interface clashes, ensuring a 100% clash-free federated model prior to fabrication.
*   **Fabrication Drawing Packages:** Produced detailed facade layout coordination sheets, unit fabrication drawings (including CNC cut schedules such as panel CODE: SSLY-06), enlarged bracket details, and shop drawings aligned with client and consultant deliverable requirements.

### 3. The Deliverable & Impact
*   **Fabrication-Ready Facade Model:** Delivered an accurate, clash-free BIM model of all rainscreen facade cladding walls fully integrated with structural supports.
*   **Zero Re-work During Assembly:** Detailed shop drawing packages and CNC panel schedules eliminated on-site fabrication conflicts and accelerated envelope closure.

## Project 4: Wynn – Marjan Island
**Location:** Ras Al Khaimah, UAE  
**Sector:** Luxury Hospitality / Complex Facade BIM  
**Role:** BIM Engineer  
**Tools Used:** Autodesk Revit (Parametric Families), Navisworks Manage, ACC, IFC / openBIM, AutoCAD  

### 1. The Context & Challenge
For the world-class Wynn Al Marjan Island luxury resort mega-development, I delivered specialized BIM modeling and coordination focused on complex facade engineering. The challenge involved engineering intricate parametric facade assemblies—including corniced parapet mouldings, mitred corner joints, extruded coping profiles, stepped window surrounds, and curved canopy panels—that conformed strictly to multi-consultant IFC openBIM specifications while maintaining exact geometric tolerances for fabrication and site installation.

### 2. My Process & Technical Execution
*   **Complex Parametric Facade Families:** Built robust nested parametric Revit families for decorative envelope profiles, cornices, aerodynamic canopy/louvre panels, and window surround cladding, ensuring parametric flex and fabrication parameter integration.
*   **Multi-Discipline Clash Resolution:** Coordinated federated architectural, facade, and structural models in Navisworks Manage, resolving clearances for cantilever structural steel brackets, cleat fixings, and anchor bolts prior to fabrication.
*   **IFC openBIM Interoperability:** Managed model exchanges with international consultant teams, ensuring high-fidelity geometric and property data transfers.
*   **Documentation & Coordination Sheets:** Produced comprehensive colonnade and portico coordination drawings, sectional details with fixing brackets, and fabrication schedules.

### 3. The Deliverable & Impact
*   **Fabrication-Accurate Envelope Model:** Delivered fully coordinated, clash-free facade models and parametric family libraries integrated into the federated project environment.
*   **Zero Assembly Conflicts:** Rigorous bracket and clearance coordination prevented on-site re-work and accelerated facade envelope assembly.

*   **Comprehensive Drawing Packages:** Under my leadership, the team successfully produced detailed layout sheets and shop drawings directly from these complex families, flawlessly bridging the gap between design intent and manufacturing.

# BIM Automation & Computational Design Spotlight

## Tool: Batch Sheet Revision Date Manager
* **Environment:** Autodesk Revit 2021 + Dynamo (IronPython 2 / Revit API)
* **Problem Solved:** During formal submissions, updating revision dates across dozens or hundreds of sheets manually is repetitive, time-consuming, and error-prone.
* **Solution:** Custom selection-based Dynamo visual script with an integrated Python node that reads selected `ViewSheet` elements directly from Revit's Project Browser, validates date inputs, supports preview mode, and commits batch updates atomically using `TransactionManager`.
* **Deliverables / Snips:**
  1. Dynamo visual script graph (Inputs, Python Node, Output results)
  2. Revit Python script editor showing custom IronPython 2 / RevitAPI code
  3. Automated Revit title block revision schedule table output (`Rev A - ISSUED FOR APPROVAL - 16-09-2026`)
